Privacy policy

Hearthbound has no account, no ads and no analytics, and collects no personal data. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what Google Play handles when you buy the full game, and how this website works.

1. Who we are

Hearthbound is made and published by Ming Zhu, an individual developer, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy. "We" and "us" below mean the developer.

For privacy questions or requests, contact ming.life@foxmail.com.

2. The short version

  • The game has no account and no sign-in, and we run no server for it.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use an advertising ID.
  • Your colonies, settings and achievements are saved only on your phone.
  • If you buy the full game, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because the game does not collect any.

3. What stays on your phone

The game keeps these in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your colony. The saved game, written every in-game day and whenever the app goes to the background.
  • Your settings. Language, text size, haptics, reduced motion, left-hand mode, battery saver, night darkness and when to pause.
  • Your Legacy. Past colonies, achievements and what you unlocked, and the Founder and heirloom chosen for your next colony.
  • Whether you own the full game. A copy of Google Play's last answer, so the game works offline.

None of this is sent to us. The names in the game belong to its fictional settlers; the game never asks for your name or any other personal information.

Android backup. If you have turned on backup for your Google account, Android may include the game's saved data in your device backup and restore it on a new phone. Google stores that backup under its own privacy policy. We cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

4. Buying the full game

Hearthbound is free to download, and the first year of every colony is free. The full game is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing.

  • Payment. Google Play takes the payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • Checking ownership. Each time the game starts or comes back from the background, it asks the Google Play app on your phone whether your Google account owns the full game, and what it costs in your currency. Play answers with the purchase's status and a purchase token. The game uses them on your phone to unlock the full game and to confirm the purchase to Google Play (Play refunds a purchase that is not confirmed). It does not send them to us.
  • Order records. Like every Google Play developer, we can see Google's records of orders for the full game in Google Play Console (such as the order number, product, price, date and country). We use them only for accounting, refunds and support.

5. Permissions and connections

The game asks for no runtime permissions: not your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The app lists two install-time permissions, both required by Google's Play Billing Library:

  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING), to offer the full game for sale.
  • Network access (INTERNET). The game itself sends nothing over the network. Google's billing library uses this permission and may send diagnostic information about billing to Google, under Google's privacy policy.

6. This website and email

This website is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own, stores nothing in your browser, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. GitHub, as the host, receives technical information needed to serve the pages, such as your IP address and browser, and may keep it in security logs under the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. We delete support emails once they are no longer needed to help you, or sooner if you ask.

7. Deleting your data, and your rights

  • On your phone. To delete everything the game stores, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Hearthbound → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall it. Starting a new colony from the Colony panel replaces the current save. Deleting the game's data cannot be undone.
  • Your purchase. Clearing data or uninstalling does not remove a purchase. It belongs to your Google account, and the game finds it again the next time it asks Google Play. Refunds go through Google Play (see Support).
  • With us. We hold no account or game data about you. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. For a request about the emails you sent us, or the order records described in section 4, write to ming.life@foxmail.com.

8. Children

Hearthbound is made for teenagers and adults, not for children under 13. It shows fights with raiders and wild animals, and settlers can die. The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game's handling of data changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that sends data off your phone, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and ask for your consent in the game where the law requires it.
