Support

Answers about the free year, the full game, your saved colonies and the settings, and how to reach us.

Contact us

For a problem with the game, a purchase, or a privacy request, email ming.life@foxmail.com.

Please include your phone model, its Android version, the game's version (on the game's Google Play page, under About this app), and what happened. A screenshot helps. For a purchase, add the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) from your receipt email. We answer as soon as we can, usually within a few days.

The free year

The first year of every colony is free: summer, autumn and the first winter. It ends at dawn on the first day of spring, about three hours of play at normal speed. A ticker line warns you three days before, and the Colony panel shows how many days are left.

The free year never ends in the middle of a crisis. A raid, a settler who is down, or an event waiting for your answer holds it back for up to two in-game days.

When it ends, the colony is saved and paused. Nothing is taken away: you can still look round the map, read the chronicle and the settlers' sheets, visit the Legacy Hall, and start a new colony, whose first year is free too. Buy the full game to let the colony go on.

Buying the full game

Tap Unlock the full game on the card at the end of the free year, or in the Colony panel at any time. Google Play shows the price in your currency and takes the payment. Once Play confirms it, the game says "The full game is yours", and every colony can go on from then on.

It is a single purchase: no subscription, and nothing else is sold in the game. The purchase belongs to your Google account, so it also covers the game on your other Android devices signed in to the same account.

If you chose a payment method that takes time, such as cash at a shop, the Colony panel says the purchase is waiting for payment. The game unlocks when Google Play confirms it.

The full game didn't unlock

There is no restore button: the game asks Google Play whether you own the full game every time it starts or comes back to the screen. If it still says the free year is over:

  • Close the game completely and open it again.
  • Open the Play Store app and check that it is signed in to the Google account you bought with.
  • If the game says Google Play can't be reached, check your connection and that the Play Store app is up to date, then try again.
  • Check your email for the Google Play receipt. If there is none, the payment may not have gone through.

If none of this helps, email us with the order number from the receipt.

Refunds

Purchases go through Google Play, so refunds do too. Within 48 hours of buying, you can request one yourself through Google Play (Google's refund help). After that, email us with the order number and we will look at your request.

A refunded purchase locks the full game again the next time the game checks with Google Play. Your colonies stay saved.

Your saved colony

The game saves your colony on your phone every in-game day and whenever you leave the app, and picks up where you left off when you open it again. Nothing is saved online, and there is no sync between devices.

  • Starting a new colony (Colony panel) replaces the current one. To keep its story in the Legacy Hall, retire it first with Retire this colony on the Charter sheet.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes your colony and Legacy. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may restore them when you reinstall or move to a new phone.
  • Your purchase is never lost this way: it belongs to your Google account.

Settings and accessibility

Open Colony on the bottom bar, then Settings:

Language
English or Simplified Chinese. The game follows your phone's language until you choose.
Text size
Makes every label in the game larger.
Left-hand mode
Moves the speed button, the sheets' Close button and the ideas badge to the left.
Reduced motion
The camera jumps instead of gliding, and sheets snap instead of sliding.
Haptics
A short buzz when an urgent alert appears or an event card opens.
Night
How dark the map gets after dusk: full, soft or off.
Battery saver
On by default. The game draws 30 frames a second instead of 60 while the colony runs, so the phone stays cooler. The colony runs at the same pace.
Pausing
Choose whether a death, a Lesson or an event card pauses the game. If you let time run, the settlers answer events themselves after a few hours.

Controls

  • Every control is a single tap. Tap a settler, a building or the ground to see what it is; tap Nudge on a settler's sheet and then a target to send them there.
  • Drag to move round the map and pinch to zoom. Pause and the speed button (1x, 2x, 3x) are on the bottom bar.
  • Android's back button or gesture closes the open sheet, cancels a tool or returns from a site's map. With nothing open, it leaves the game, which saves first.
